# Update 2.2.1

### What's New

- The music manager has a cleaner and smoother design.
- Online music now shows its download progress while it is being prepared.
- The theme colour wheel is smoother and changes colours faster.
- Error messages are now easier to understand and include an error code.
- Modpacks and installed mods have received improvements.

### Bug Fixes

- Fixed online music not playing properly in some cases.
- Fixed an error that could happen when Play was clicked many times while online music was downloading.
- Fixed music playlists, artwork, and installed mods not updating immediately sometimes.
- Fixed music drag handles, sidebar alignment, and editor controls.
- Improved launcher performance and fixed many small issues.
